Youthful Tiger and Yarrow

Meet the Superhero Team

Youthful Tiger is a brave and energetic superhero with the strength and speed of a tiger. Yarrow is the loyal sidekick, using the healing and protective powers of the yarrow plant to help the team. Together, they protect their city from dangerous villains.

Our Foes

  • Thunder Fang
    • Major Power: Controls powerful thunderstorms.
    • Minor Power: Can make small sparks appear from his hands.
  • Shadow Frost
    • Major Power: Can freeze objects with ice.
    • Minor Power: Can lower the temperature around her.
